From a physics degree to finding patterns in data.

I'm Priyanka De, a data analyst and aspiring data scientist with a BSc in Physics Honors. Physics trained me to break complex systems into testable questions, and that habit now shapes how I work with data — forming a hypothesis, checking it against evidence, and building something reliable from what I find.

Since graduating, I've moved deliberately into data work: building SQL and Python foundations, applying them during a hands-on data analytics apprenticeship, and now going deeper into machine learning and data science through structured coursework. Along the way I've picked up Power BI, statistics, and storytelling as the tools that turn analysis into something people can actually act on.
